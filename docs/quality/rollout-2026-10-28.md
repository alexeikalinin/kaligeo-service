# Question quality rollout — 2026-10-28

Published to app.kaligeo.by and Trigger.dev production.

## Measurement and review

- Site evidence → business map → administrator approval → question generation → annotator and independent critic → administrator approval → platform requests.
- Approval hashes bind exact map and question content; edits invalidate critique/approval. Optimistic updates reject stale editors.
- An administrator can edit facts, audiences, relationships, aliases and scope; annotate questions; apply suggested replacements; selectively rewrite questions; review again.
- Feedback is saved and supplied to subsequent checks in the same audit. This is contextual feedback, not model fine-tuning or a cross-client curated training library.
- Telegram notification delivery state is recorded. Cabinet approval remains required.
- Company, owned-product and seller measurements are separate, with response excerpts. Literal approved-name detection measures mentions, not recommendations, sentiment or causal factors.
- Summary index is the share of spontaneous responses mentioning their approved target. Direct brand questions remain separate. Incompatible older summary widgets are excluded for this methodology.
- HTML/PDF use entity measurements. Basic and Monitor Start now include PDF and up to three observed alternatives, fulfilling the basic landing promise. Alternatives require answer excerpts; this is not a verified market census.

## Monitoring and recovery

- The first monitoring snapshot reuses the existing corresponding audit without model calls.
- Recurring measurements inherit approved map, questions, cards and platforms; changing them starts a new measurement series.
- History includes only matching measurement versions, questions and platforms; copied baselines are deduplicated.
- Successful answers are persisted immediately; retries request missing answers. Multi-call growth-plan and website-fix stages now checkpoint individual successful outputs.
- Historical Astrum QA reports remain available with a methodology warning, and cannot seed the new measurement series. No new client audit was launched.
